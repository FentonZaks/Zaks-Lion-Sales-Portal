# Business Rules
1. Authentication requires an approved email list (mix of @zaksfoods.ca and @gmail.com) and 30-day 2FA refresh.
2. Shared Territories: Multiple reps can be assigned to the same customer.
3. Price Overrides: Allowed, but require a comment. No formal approval required.
4. Freight is excluded from frontend totals.
5. Taxes are deferred to NetSuite (display "Plus all applicable taxes").
6. Orders must be broken down to the individual item level for NetSuite CSV, even if entered as Kits/Groups.
