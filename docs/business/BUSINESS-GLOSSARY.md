# Business Glossary
- **Order Request:** A draft order submitted by a rep, not yet in NetSuite.
- **Item Group / Kit:** A predefined bundle of items in NetSuite that acts as a single orderable SKU but has individual components.
- **Inner Pack / Carton:** Store-level unit of measure.
- **Master Case:** Distributor-level unit of measure.
- **Batch CSV:** A single export file containing multiple approved Order Requests for NetSuite import.
