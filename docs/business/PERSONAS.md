# Personas

## 1. Field Sales Representative
**Role:** Primary user of the portal. Manages assigned customers, logs visits, records phone calls, prepares order requests.
**Needs:** Fast mobile access, minimal typing, territory-restricted access, access to past 2 years of sales history.
**Constraints:** Can override prices but must provide comments. Cannot approve orders for integration.

## 2. Regional Sales Manager
**Role:** Oversees sales reps in a specific region or nationally. 
**Needs:** View all customer activity in the full organization, monitor rep performance, review submitted orders.

## 3. Head of Sales / Executive Viewer
**Role:** Strategic oversight. 
**Needs:** Aggregated reporting, high-level dashboards, no operational editing functions.

## 4. Order Entry Employee
**Role:** Internal reviewer.
**Needs:** Review submitted order requests, clarify details, prepare batch CSV for NetSuite import, record NetSuite SO number in portal.

## 5. Portal Administrator
**Role:** System manager.
**Needs:** Manage the authorized Excel sheet mapping for users, handle role assignments, configure settings.

## 6. NetSuite Administrator
**Role:** ERP manager.
**Needs:** Monitor synchronization logs, ensure kit/item group integrity.
