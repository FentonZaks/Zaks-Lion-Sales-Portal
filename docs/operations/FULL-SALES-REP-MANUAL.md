# Zaks Sales Portal: Complete Sales Representative Manual

Welcome to the Zaks Sales Portal! This CRM and dashboard tool is designed specifically for you to track your customers, log store visits, monitor your sales performance, and manage your day-to-day activities all from your laptop or mobile device.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Logging In
1. Navigate to the Sales Portal URL provided by your administrator.
2. Enter your assigned email address and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

### Resetting Your Password
If you forget your password or need to set it for the first time:
1. On the login screen, click **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your email address and submit.
3. Check your email for a secure reset link. Click the link to securely choose a new password.

---

## 2. The Sales Dashboard
When you log in, you will land on your **Sales Dashboard**. This is your personalized command center.

**What you will see:**
* **Key Metrics:** At-a-glance cards showing your total customers, Year-to-Date (YTD) Revenue, Month-to-Date (MTD) Revenue, and Total Open Accounts Receivable (Balances).
* **Revenue Charts:** Visual graphs breaking down your monthly performance so you can track trends over the year.
* **Category Distribution:** See exactly which product categories are driving your sales (e.g., Grocery, Frozen, Snacks).
* **Pending Follow-ups:** A quick-access list of all your open tasks and reminders. Clicking on any follow-up will take you directly to that customer to complete the task.

---

## 3. The Customers Page
Click the **Customers** tab in the main navigation to view your assigned territory.

**Features of this page:**
* **Global Search:** Use the search bar to instantly find a customer by their Store Name or NetSuite ID.
* **Banner Filter:** Filter your list to only show stores belonging to a specific banner (e.g., all 7-Eleven stores).
* **Sorting Data:** You can click on the column headers (MTD Rev, YTD Rev, Balance, Last Activity) to sort the list highest-to-lowest or lowest-to-highest. This is extremely useful for finding accounts that haven't been visited recently or owe large balances.
* **Quick Stats:** You can immediately see if a store has an open follow-up task right from the main list.

---

## 4. The Customer Profile Screen
Clicking on any customer from the list opens their detailed **Customer Profile**. This is where you do all of your actual logging and account management.

### Top Metrics & Information
At the top of the profile, you will see the store's address, NetSuite internal ID, pricing tier, and critical live metrics pulled directly from NetSuite (MTD, YTD, Balance, and Last Invoice Date).

### Logging Activities (Calls, Store Visits, Emails)
The Activity Log is your timeline of interactions with the store.
1. Scroll down to the **Log Activity** section.
2. Select the **Type** of interaction (Call, Store Visit, Email, Meeting).
3. Type out your detailed notes in the text box.
4. Click **Log Activity**. 
*Note: This automatically updates the "Last Activity Date" on the main customer list so you know exactly when you last touched this account.*

### Setting Follow-Ups & Reminders
Never miss a promise to a store.
1. Under the **Add Follow-up** section, write what needs to be done (e.g., "Check if the new display rack arrived").
2. Select a **Due Date**.
3. Click **Add Follow-up**.
4. This task will now appear as `PENDING` on your main Dashboard.
5. Once you complete the task, find it in the customer's profile and click **Mark Complete**.

### Adding Store Pictures
Visual proof is critical for merchandising and layout tracking.
1. Under the **Store Pictures** section, click **Upload Picture**.
2. Select a photo from your phone's camera roll or your computer.
3. Add a short description (e.g., "New front end cap setup").
4. Click **Upload**. The picture is instantly saved to the database and can be viewed by anyone accessing this profile.

### Viewing Order History
Need to know what they bought last week? Click the **Order History** tab inside the profile to see a live, line-by-line breakdown of their past NetSuite invoices and orders, including quantities and exact pricing.

---

## 5. Requesting a New Customer
*(For detailed instructions on this process, see the standalone **New Customer Creation Guide**).*
1. Navigate to the **New Customer** tab.
2. Fill out the application completely, ensuring you select your name as the Sales Rep.
3. Submit the request. It is automatically sent to the Admin team for approval and creation in NetSuite. Once approved, the store will appear in your Customers list.
