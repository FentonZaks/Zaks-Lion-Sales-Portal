# Zaks / Lion Sales Portal - Representative Training Guide

Welcome to the new Field Sales Portal!

## 1. Getting Started
- **Access:** Navigate to `sales.zaksfoods.ca`.
- **Login:** Use your approved email address and password.
- **Security:** You will be prompted to set up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your first login. You must refresh this 2FA code every 30 days.

## 2. Navigating the Dashboard
Your dashboard provides a quick snapshot of your monthly sales performance, overdue follow-ups, and a searchable list of your assigned customers.

## 3. Managing Customers & Activities
- Click on a customer to view their **Profile** (payment terms, balances).
- Use the **Activity Forms** to log a Visit, Call, or Note. 
- All logged activities will appear in the chronological **Activity Timeline**.

## 4. Drafting an Order
1. Select a customer and click **Draft Order**.
2. Browse the **Product Catalog** to add items. Note the estimated inventory availability!
3. **Prices:** You are permitted to override product prices. However, if you change a price, you **must** enter a comment explaining the reason before the system will allow you to submit the order.
4. **Totals:** The subtotal shown on the portal excludes freight and taxes. 
5. Click **Submit Order Request**. It will be sent to the internal Order Entry team for review.
