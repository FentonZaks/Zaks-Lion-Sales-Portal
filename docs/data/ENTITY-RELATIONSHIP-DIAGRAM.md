# ERD Overview
- Users (Auth)
- Customers (Synced from NetSuite)
- UserCustomers (Many-to-Many mapping for territory)
- Products (Items & Kit definitions synced from NetSuite)
- Prices (Synced)
- OrderRequests (Drafts, Submissions)
- OrderRequestLines (Breakdown of kits to individual components)
- Activities (Visits, Calls, Notes)
